# Business, Finance and Consulting Careers

## Consulting
Management consultants help organisations solve problems. The work is structured
problem-solving, analysis, stakeholder work and communication.
- **Skills:** structured thinking (issue trees), Excel modelling, slide writing, client
  communication, comfort with ambiguity.
- **Entry routes:** graduate analyst programmes at large firms, boutique consultancies,
  internal strategy/strategy-and-operations roles.
- **Progression:** analyst -> consultant -> manager -> principal/partner.
- **Trade-offs:** steep learning curve and strong exit options, but long hours and travel.

## Finance
Finance splits into several families:
- **Investment Banking (IB):** M&A and capital raising; heavy modelling and deal execution.
- **Sales & Trading:** markets, pricing and client execution.
- **Asset / Investment Management:** research and portfolio management.
- **Corporate Finance / FP&A:** budgeting, forecasting and decision support inside a company.
- **Risk and Compliance:** managing financial and regulatory risk.
- **FinTech:** applying technology to financial products.
- **Skills:** accounting, financial modelling (DCF, comps), Excel, valuation, attention to
  detail, regulatory awareness. Quantitative roles add statistics and programming.

## Marketing
- **Brand and Content Marketing:** positioning, campaigns, storytelling.
- **Performance / Growth Marketing:** paid acquisition, funnels, analytics, A/B testing.
- **Product Marketing:** connecting product features to customer value and sales enablement.
- **Skills:** analytics (GA4, SQL helpful), copywriting, experimentation, customer insight.

## Sales
- **SDR/BDR (entry):** prospecting and qualifying; the common entry point.
- **Account Executive:** owning deals and quotas.
- **Sales Engineering / Solutions:** technical pre-sales with a lighter coding load.
- **Customer Success:** retaining and growing accounts.
- **Skills:** resilience, communication, negotiation, CRM discipline (Salesforce/HubSpot).

## Operations and Supply Chain
- **Ops Analyst / Manager:** process improvement LEAN/Six Sigma, logistics, inventory.
- **Skills:** process mapping, data analysis, cross-functional coordination, ERP systems.

## Human Resources (People)
- **Talent Acquisition, HR Business Partner, L&D, People Analytics.**
- **Skills:** communication, empathy, employment law basics, analytics.

## Project and Program Management
- **Skills:** planning, stakeholder management, risk management, delivery. Certifications
  such as PMP or PRINCE2 can help in some industries.

## Positioning for business roles from a non-business background
Technical graduates are attractive in:
- **Product management**, **product marketing**, **sales engineering**, **fintech**,
  **technical consulting** and **business/operations analytics**.
Frame your degree as evidence of analytical ability and your projects as evidence of
initiative. Add one "business" proof point: a case competition, a market analysis, a
volunteer project, or a relevant certification.

## Practical certifications (optional, not mandatory)
- Analytics / Excel / SQL certificates for analyst roles.
- CFA (long, rigorous) for investment roles; CPA for accounting; PMP for project management.
- Most business roles value demonstrated work and internships over certificates.
